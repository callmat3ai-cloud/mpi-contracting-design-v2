Assets and functionality

The text wordmark is a temporary stand-in for approved logo artwork. Approved project images and client testimonials are pending. Display and text faces are self-hosted: Bodoni Moda and Archivo, both under the SIL Open Font License.

The form demonstrates local validation only. No information is submitted, saved or transmitted. No analytics or tracking are installed, and no third-party requests are made at runtime. All pages are noindex.

Motion

Scroll reveals, image parallax and the hero field are progressive enhancements. They are disabled entirely under prefers-reduced-motion, and every page reads completely with JavaScript switched off.
